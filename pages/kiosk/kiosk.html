WHAT IS THE SECOND REALM?

Bitcoin isn't just code, it's a territory.

For most people, the Bitcoin blockchain is merely a synonym for boring financial transactions and abstract strings of characters that might make you rich someday.

But this grim vision is far from inevitable. Behind the curtain lies another world.

The Second Realm is designed to make the structure of Bitcoin data more visible to anyone. Inside each Realm, the blockchain appears as a points cloud. Each point is a block. Each axis of the world is assigned to a Bitcoin metric: block height, transactions count, block size, etc.

And from that data, landscapes emerge.

HOW TO PILOT THE DATASHIP

Buttons

  • Trigger button: Horizontal translation (forward)
  • Button B/Y: Increase the speed mode
  • Button A/X: Decrease the speed mode
  • Meta Button (long press): Recenter the camera

 

Thumbstick

  • Up: Vertical Translation (up)
  • Down: Vertical Translation (down)
  • Left: Rotation Left (Anticlockwise rotation around vertical axis)
  • Right: Rotation Right (Clockwise rotation around vertical axis)
